# Pricing Research

## Competitor Pricing

### SortFeed (Chrome Extension - Direct Competitor)
- Free: 25 posts sorting
- Pro: $14/mo unlimited sorting, exporting, downloading
- One-time: $39.99 for lifetime

### Flick (Social Media Tool)
- Solo: £11/mo (~$14/mo) - basics
- Pro: £24/mo (~$30/mo) - time-saving features
- Agency: Higher tier for teams

### Later (Social Media Scheduler)
- Starter: $25/mo - 1 social set, 30 posts/mo
- Growth: $45/mo - 3 social sets, analytics, 150 posts
- Advanced: $80/mo - 6 social sets, unlimited posts
- Agency: $200/mo - 15 social sets

### Metricool
- Free: 1 brand, all social accounts
- Starter: $18-25/mo (5 brands)
- Advanced: $45-67/mo (more brands)
- Custom: Enterprise

## SaaS Pricing Psychology Insights
1. Annual vs Monthly: 20-30% revenue increase when offering both with appropriate discounts
2. Monthly has 50% higher initial conversion but annual has far better retention
3. Standard annual discount: 20-40% off monthly equivalent
4. "Save X%" badge on annual toggle is most effective
5. Highlight the "most popular" middle tier
6. Use decoy pricing - make the middle tier look like best value
7. Anchor with expensive tier first (or show it prominently)
8. Free tier should be useful but create desire for more
9. Feature gating should feel natural, not punishing

## Revenue Model for $1M in 2026
- Target: $83,333/mo MRR by Dec 2026
- At $49/mo avg (mix of tiers): ~1,700 paying customers
- At $29/mo avg: ~2,875 paying customers
- With 60% annual: better cash flow, lower churn

## Proposed Tier Structure
- Free: Hook users, limited but useful
- Starter ($19/mo): Individual creators getting started
- Pro ($49/mo): Serious creators - MOST POPULAR (decoy target)
- Business ($99/mo): Teams and agencies
- Agency ($199/mo): Large agencies, white-label

## Annual Pricing Strategy
- 40% discount on annual = "Save 40%" badge
- Free: $0
- Starter: $19/mo → $11/mo annual ($132/yr vs $228/yr)
- Pro: $49/mo → $29/mo annual ($348/yr vs $588/yr)  
- Business: $99/mo → $59/mo annual ($708/yr vs $1188/yr)
- Agency: $199/mo → $119/mo annual ($1428/yr vs $2388/yr)
