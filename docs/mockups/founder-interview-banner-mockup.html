What happens in SpyReels

  1. 1 Popup auto-opens ~400ms after page load if user has 2+ scripts and has not dismissed it.
  2. 2 User selects country + phone. Book stays disabled until the number is valid (6 to 15 digits).
  3. 3 Book a 15-minute call saves the phone to SpyReels, notifies the founder (Telegram + owner notification), then opens Calendly with name, email, and phone pre-filled.
  4. 4 Not now or X closes the modal. “Not now” never shows again. X / clicking outside hides it until the next visit (new session).
  5. 5 Original SpyReels tab stays on Coach or Dashboard. User can keep working immediately.

What happens on Calendly (new tab)

  1. 1 Scheduling page loads at calendly.com/noman-peera/spyreels-feedback (placeholder URL you can swap later).
  2. 3 User picks a date and time from your available slots (15-minute event). Name, email, and phone may already be filled from SpyReels.
  3. 4 User confirms any remaining Calendly questions you configure.
  4. 5 Calendly confirms the booking and sends calendar invites to you and the user. You also received their phone in SpyReels when they clicked Book.
  5. 6 On call day you join via the video link in the calendar invite (Zoom/Google Meet per your Calendly setup).
