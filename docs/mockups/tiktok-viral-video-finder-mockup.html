What is a TikTok finder?

A TikTok finder (also called a TikTok video finder or TikTok viral video finder) is a free tool that helps you find TikTok videos that already work. SpyReels is built for the by username job: paste any public TikTok handle or profile link, then sort that creator's videos by views, engagement rate and outlier score (how far a video beat that account's own median). You get the winning hooks in text, then remake any of them in your own voice as a script with production notes.

  • By username, not keyword chaos. Keyword libraries show random viral clips. Spying a peer account shows formats you can actually copy.
  • Outlier score, not raw views. A 200K TikTok on a 5K account beats a 2M TikTok on a mega-creator when you are still growing.

Key TikTok terms, explained

Short definitions so the metrics on this TikTok finder make sense.

For You Page (FYP)

The feed where TikTok surfaces videos to people who do not follow you. Most virality starts here.

Outlier score

How many times a video beat that account's own median views. The signal SpyReels sorts by first.

Hook

The first 1 to 3 seconds that stop the scroll. Study hooks across outliers before you invent a new one.

Repurposing

Keeping the proven structure and rewriting it in your voice. Not re-uploading someone else's video.
