## The honest limits of any instagram reels analytics tool

Public data is not private data. You cannot see a competitor’s exact retention curve unless they share it. You cannot see their funnel unless you infer it from CTAs and landing behavior. That is still a goldmine if you use it like a strategist, not a stalker.
