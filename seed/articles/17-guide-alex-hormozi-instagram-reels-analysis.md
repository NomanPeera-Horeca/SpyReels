## How to remake Hormozi-style Reels ethically

1. Pick one **hook pattern** (contrarian or direct promise).
2. Match **format** (talking head or text on screen for opinions).
3. Write **thumbnail text** as a headline before you film.
4. Deliver one concrete insight in under 60 to 90 seconds unless the hook earns longer.
5. Use [script generator guide](/blog/guide-instagram-reels-script-generator) for your niche angle.

Do not copy personal stories or unique proof. Copy pacing, format, and hook **type**.
