Instagram Reels hook patterns are repeatable opening structures (question, stat, contrarian, direct promise, story, listicle, and others) that set tension in the first three seconds. We labeled hook patterns on 308 of 420 top-performing Reels from 14 creators (June 2026 export, ranked by engagement rate). Question hooks averaged the highest engagement rate in this sample; direct promise and contrarian hooks appeared most often; listicle hooks averaged the lowest ER among common patterns.

## Why hook patterns beat random "viral hooks"

A hook pattern is a **template**, not a sentence. "Contrarian" might sound like "Stop posting daily" or "Most coaches price this wrong." The pattern is the tension type; the words change per niche.

Studying patterns across hundreds of rows shows what **top performers already filtered by ER** used at the open. That is stronger than copying one Reel you saw on Explore.

## Pattern 1: Question hooks

**Shape:** Ask or imply a question in line one. Often challenges a default behavior.

**Example angle from top performers:** "Run clubs are boring. Someone should try this…" (question + contrarian blend).

**When to use:** Market gaps, product ideas, myth busting.

**Filming tip:** Deliver the question in under 2 seconds. No "hey guys" warm-up.

## Pattern 2: Stat and number hooks

**Shape:** Lead with a specific number, dollar amount, or time frame.

**Example angle:** "Around $44,000 a month, we pay for food…" (restaurant cost breakdown Reel).

**When to use:** Educational business content, proof-heavy niches.

**Filming tip:** Put the number in the first sentence and on thumbnail text if possible.

## Pattern 3: Contrarian hooks

**Shape:** Attack a common belief or practice.

**Example angles:** "Apple's most secret device just leaked…" (tech contrarian curiosity). Hormozi-family tops skew contrarian.

**When to use:** Opinions, frameworks, business coaching.

**Filming tip:** Pair with talking head or bold text on screen.

Hormozi data: [Alex Hormozi Reels analysis](/blog/guide-alex-hormozi-instagram-reels-analysis).

## Pattern 4: Direct promise hooks

**Shape:** Promise a specific outcome or list.

**Example angles:** "This guy came up with a genius business idea…" (story + promise). "Seven menu items every profitable restaurant has…"

**When to use:** Tutorials, breakdowns, case studies.

**Filming tip:** One promise only. Cut secondary offers from line one.

## Pattern 5: Story hooks

**Shape:** Start in a moment ("Christmas Eve, I was…", "picture of me at rock bottom").

**When to use:** Personal proof before a lesson.

**Filming tip:** Story must pivot to a teachable point within 15 seconds or retention drops.

## Pattern 6: Listicle hooks (caution)

Listicles appeared often but averaged **lowest ER** among major patterns in our tagged set. Weak listicles open with "5 tips" without tension. Strong listicles still lead with a **number + stakes** ("Tell if a restaurant is dirty in 30 seconds…").

1. Export competitor top 10 by [engagement rate](/blog/guide-instagram-reels-engagement-rate).

3. Pick **one pattern** for your next two weeks of filming.
4. Write three opens using that pattern before you touch format or b-roll.

## Frequently asked questions

### What are the most common Instagram Reels hook patterns?

In our 2026 sample, direct promise and contrarian were most common among top performers. Questions and stats averaged higher engagement rate.

### What is the best hook pattern for Reels?

There is no universal best. Questions averaged 5.67% ER in our tagged set, but sample sizes vary. Test patterns against competitors in **your** niche.

### How is this different from hook examples lists?

Examples give sentences. This study tags **patterns** across 420 ER-ranked Reels so you choose structure first.

### How do I tag hook patterns manually?

Read the first 3 seconds. Label: question, stat, contrarian, direct promise, story, listicle, comparison, or confession. Keep a spreadsheet column.

### Do hook patterns work on TikTok?

Patterns transfer. Metrics and culture differ. Validate on each platform.
