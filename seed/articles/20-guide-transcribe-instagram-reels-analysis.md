## Why transcribe competitor Reels at all

Views tell you what reached people. Transcripts tell you **what was said**, in order, with timing. Without transcription you guess hooks from memory after one watch.

Transcription enables:

- Hook text extraction (first ~3.5 seconds)
- Hook pattern labeling (contrarian, stat, question, etc.)
- Full script pacing for remakes
- Hook analysis and remakeability scoring

Download-only tools (common with basic viral finders) skip this layer.

## Manual workflow: transcribe and analyze without software

1. **Pick 3 to 5 public competitors.**
2. **Save 10 to 30 top Reels each** (by your best guess of performance or Instagram's sort).
3. **Transcribe hooks** using CapCut auto-captions, Whisper, or Otter on screen recordings.
4. **Build a spreadsheet** with columns: URL, views, likes, comments, ER (calculate from followers), hook text, pattern, format, topic.
5. **Sort by ER** and look for repeats in the top 10 rows.

Budget 2 to 4 hours for 30 Reels manual. Automation cuts that to minutes on refresh.

## Ethics and limits

- Only **public** Reels you can already view.
- Transcribe for **research and original remakes**, not reposting someone else's script verbatim.
- Transcription quality varies with music, accents, and noise. Re-listen to disputed hooks.
- You cannot see private Insights (skip rate, watch time) on competitors.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I transcribe Instagram Reels for free?

Use CapCut auto-captions, YouTube upload + auto subs, or Whisper on a local recording. Manual but workable for small batches.

### Does Instagram transcribe Reels automatically?

Instagram uses audio for search in some cases, but does not give you an exportable competitor transcript. You need your own pipeline.

### What is hook text vs full transcript?

Hook text is the opening few seconds. Full transcript is the entire Reel. Research both; thumbnail text is a third layer on the cover.

### Can I export transcripts to Excel?

Yes. SpyReels exports .xlsx with transcript, hook text, and classification columns. Manual workflow uses CSV you build yourself.

### Is transcribing competitor Reels legal?

Studying public content for research is standard. Copying unique expression or reposting without rights is not.

### What hook patterns should I tag after transcribing?

Start with question, stat, contrarian, direct promise, story, and listicle. See [hook patterns data](/blog/guide-instagram-reels-hook-patterns-data).
