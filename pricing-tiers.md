## Decoy Pricing Psychology
- Free tier: Useful enough to get hooked, limited enough to feel constrained
- Starter: Entry point, but missing key features (AI cues, voice teleprompter, Google Docs)
- Pro (MOST POPULAR): Sweet spot with all individual features, best value perception
- Business: For teams, 3x Pro price but 5x limits + team features
- Agency: Premium anchor, makes Business look reasonable

## Monthly vs Annual Push Strategy
- Default toggle position: Annual (pre-selected)
- "Save 40%" badge on annual toggle
- Monthly prices shown with strikethrough on annual view
- "Most Popular" badge on Pro annual
- "Best Value" badge on annual toggle
- Show annual as "per month, billed annually" (lower number)
- Countdown timer: "Annual pricing expires in X days" (urgency)
