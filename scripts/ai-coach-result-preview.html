The Silent Profit Killer Hiding in Every Restaurant Kitchen

Voiceover + B-Roll Instagram Reels TOFU 45-60 seconds
H
Hook

First 2 seconds. Stop the scroll. No warm-up.

Most restaurants fail not because of bad food, but because of one invisible line item on their P&L that nobody talks about until it is too late.
Try 3 alternative hooks
E
Explain

Name the problem. Show it is bigger than they think. Build tension. Do not solve it yet.

Commercial kitchen equipment is the second-largest expense after labor, and most owners have no idea what their walk-in, fryers, and hood systems actually cost to run every month. In 2026, with commercial energy rates up 18% year over year, that blind spot is quietly eating 8 to 12 percent of gross revenue at independent restaurants.
I
Illustrate

One specific story or example. Concrete details so they see themselves in it.

I walked into a client's kitchen last month. Beautiful setup. New line, proper ventilation. Owner was proud. Then we pulled his utility bills. His walk-in alone was running $1,400 a month because the door seal was shot and nobody had checked it in two years. One $40 part. Fourteen hundred dollars a month bleeding out.
T
Teach

ONE clear takeaway. Not a list. One thing they should do differently.

Pull your last three utility bills and divide total kWh by total covers served. That number is your real cost per guest. If it is above $0.80, your equipment stack is working against you, not for you.
CTA
Call to Action

Tell them exactly what to do next. One thing only.

Comment ENERGY and I will send you the free equipment audit checklist we use on every new restaurant install.
